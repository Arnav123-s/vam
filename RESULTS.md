### Pre-registered criteria

Both were registered for the counting law, then called "consolidating" (see the post-audit revision in `PROTOCOL.md`). Because that law is a counting rule, criterion 2 is close to true by construction.

1. Counting law within 0.01 of the window floor for k ≤ 8, every source: **pass** (largest gap -0.0014, S1 order-2 Markov bits, k = 2).
2. Counting law within 0.01 of KT or better, every source and k: **pass** (largest excess +0.0011, S1 order-2 Markov bits, k = 16).

Not pre-registered, added after the audit: the literal T12.1 law measured against criterion 1 would fail. Its largest excess over the floor is +0.0252 (S4 delayed echo d=6, k = 8), and it falls furthest below the floor by -0.1015 (S4 delayed echo d=6, k = 4). With a constant rate its weights keep moving with recent history: it settles above the floor where learning has converged, and beats the fixed-rule floor where recent history carries information the window does not.
