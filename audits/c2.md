## 4. Part 1: all criteria pass; where a layer does not do what N says

Criteria (all True): register cap 64 bits holds the deepest level; integrated ODE equals the machine (flow differences 5e-11 or less; generation
5.9e-12); `-ln q` equals the integral of `Tr(K R)` (8.9e-16); Page rank equals hidden states on S1, S2, S4, iid; coherent readout in
[2.44e-4, 0.99976]; untruncated chain engines leak 1.1e-15 outside the cone with 1.9e-27 discarded; text survival (plain 0.000;
verify 2: 0.591 of 0.600; governor: 0.651 of 0.658); hbar 0 equals the planner bit for bit; hbar 1 equals the prediction to 1.1e-16.

Do not read the following as failed criteria. They are places where no criterion caught that a layer measured nothing or did something other
than its description.

**N-L2 on text measured nothing.**
- On A-train (first 4 MiB) the Page rank is 0 at every depth 1 to 7, including the trivial direction, with cutoff 1.1 throughout
  (`part1.N_L2.text_depth_profile`). The reported turnaround of 1 byte comes from the tie-break (smallest depth), not from the data.
- Diagnosis (measured on a 4 MiB English proxy, not PG-19): the split-half edge saturates near 1 because contexts seen in only one half give a
  whitened difference of order 1 whatever the sample size. A count floor on rows and columns removes that, but the result depends on the
  floor: on the proxy, floor 50 gives ranks 0 to 3; floor 256 gives 35, 14, 48, 45, 43, 40, 36 (depths 1 to 7); floor 500 gives 35, 43, 41, 38,
  35, 31, 25; byte-shuffled null text gives rank 1 (never above) at every depth where rows remain. Ranks on filtered row sets also fall with depth
  because rows are removed, so no depth cut may be derived from them.
- The depth question is answered empirically by Part 2 stage 1: the default depths 2.0316, depths cut to `(8,)` 5.2759.
- Proposal T4.2 (a floor fixed in advance, reported as a measurement only).

**The text governor's "halt" does nothing for the chosen configuration.**
- Governed, admission needs `max(verify, 2)` sightings, which is 3 when verify is 3. Occupancy passes 0.5 and reaches 0.87 (deepest level) at the end
  of A and 0.98 after B: volume keeps expanding.
- Its measured gain (verify 3 alone 1.9346, with governor 1.8676) therefore comes from protection and sinks, not from halting growth.

**On the text machine the sinks cost accuracy at every rate tried.**
- A-validation bpb rises monotonically with the sink rate at every protect and verify setting, e.g. protect 0.5, verify 3: rate 0.1 1.8676, 0.25 1.8751,
  0.5 1.8849, 1.0 1.8957. Rate 0 (a governor with protection but no drain) was not in the grid. See T4.1.
- Reading: on text the doorkeeper already does the job N gives the sinks. Whether the drain earns anything at all is untested without rate 0.

**On the chain, `governed_page_threshold` discards 0.371 of the weight (measured).**
- The advisor's first reading was that the halt freezes the bond at the crossing. I measured it and that is not the mechanism. The discard accumulates
  before the Page point and is flat after it: cumulative discarded weight at events 10, 20, 30, 34, 40 is 0.050, 0.143, 0.250, 0.371, 0.371. With
  `halt=False` the numbers are identical. Before the threshold (event 34) the sinks are off and the cache cap (90) is reached by event 29, so the
  unsinked state is truncated; after the crossing the sinks hold it.
- `always=True` (sinks on from event 1) discards 1.5e-7 at the same cap. The Page trigger (Renyi-2 entropy at half of the register's maximal 17 bits) comes
  later than the cache fills. Fix within T: take the capacity in N-L6 as the cache-limited capacity, so the governor acts when the stored volume reaches
  half of what the cache can hold. T4.3.

**Statements to keep apart (advisor):**
- Fidelity: 6.6e-6 at governed bond 64 is not comparable with the ungoverned 5.6e-4 of `context_convergence.txt`: each is measured against its own
  machine's reference, and the runs are 120 against 93 bits. The supportable claim is that the governed machine is easier to truncate (differences
  8.5e-5, 6.6e-6, 2.0e-6 at bonds 32, 64, 90 against bond 128), not that the governor improves the plain machine. The entropy-governed bond at margin 8
  (largest bond 18, mean 10.0) gives the same bits per bit (1.0078) as bond 128 (1.0079) at a prediction difference of 6.3e-4.
- N-L4: the dynamical blanket is exact (cone leak 1.1e-15, untruncated). The statistical blanket of the state is not: the untruncated conditional
  mutual information is up to 0.042 bits per interior cell; truncation inflates it to 0.298 in the plain bond-64 engine (0.96 of the weight discarded).
- Area law (Amendment 1): the plain engine is readable only at 4 cells (discarded 2.5e-28); at 6, 8, 10 cells it discards 0.11, 0.96, 2.35. All governed rows
  are readable (discarded 6e-13 to 8.3e-7) and bounded: largest cut entropy 0.832, 0.662, 0.409, 0.301 bits at 4, 6, 8, 10 cells. That bound is
  produced by the sinks; nothing is claimed about the plain dynamics.
- Sink cost on the chain: the sinks change no prediction when the context is einselected exactly with two cells or fewer outside it (3 rows: change 0.0).
  Otherwise the largest prediction change is 0.028, 0.0075, 0.059, 0.0010; bits per bit change by at most 1.4e-4.

**T4 (within the design, each needs your dated amendment before any rerun; costs are from saved seconds):**

Chain engines only. | `governed_page_threshold` discards 0.371 before its trigger (4). | Chain blankets part: 157.9 s in total (the page-threshold engine alone 44.2 s); fidelity and area law only if the trigger is used there (it is not: they use `always=True`). |

**Diagnostic (no change to the machine):**
- D1. Split the machine's A-test code length by the length of the longest lzma-style match to the training split (or by whether the byte lies in a repeated
  8+-byte span). It tests the long-match hypothesis of 2. Cost: one frozen A-test pass of the chosen machine plus lzma bookkeeping, a few minutes
  (the frozen reading of one 4.1 MB split is within a Part 2 row's 290 s).

**For the owner, not T4 (they add a mechanism or change the design):**
- A level that sees beyond the 64-bit register: N's introduction says a sentence is a configuration of configurations of bits, so a level whose context is the
  hash of the last k completed words (or the last register's content) would be that. It is not in N-L1's register cap as written. Only worth deciding
  after D1.
- Seeds and error bars for R3 (a second seed of sentence shuffling), if the transfer question matters to the owner.
